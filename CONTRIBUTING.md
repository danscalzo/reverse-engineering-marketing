# Contributing

Thanks for helping. This is an early version, so small, concrete contributions are the most useful.

## Good contributions
- A source that changed (an ad library, a register, a platform rule): say what you read, where, and the date.
- A market the skill handles badly: country, sector, what it got wrong.
- A missed step in the method, with the prompt you used and what you expected.

## How
1. Open an issue first for anything bigger than a typo: what you asked, what it did, what you expected.
2. Keep pull requests small: one change, one reason.
3. Run the prompts in `evals/scenarios.md` that your change touches and say what happened.

## Rules for content
- No real client data, no personal data, no private account details. Examples must be fictional or fully public.
- Mark market facts `[V]` only if you read the official page; otherwise `[NV]`.
- Keep the guardrails: public sources, official APIs, read-only access. Nothing that needs logins you do not own, fake leads or scraping against a platform's terms.
- Plain English, short sentences. The skill is read by an agent every run: every line costs.
