# Market adapter: any country, any sector

The method is the same everywhere; the sources and the rules change with the market. Never assume the user's home market. Adapt to where the subject sells.

## 1. Identify the market (OBSERVED)
- Country/countries and language(s) of the site, prices and currency, phone prefixes, delivery area.
- Sector and business type (`business-type.md`).
- Regulated? (finance and credit, health, alcohol, gambling, property, employment, politics, pharma). Regulated = special ad rules and special ad categories.

## 2. Find the local equivalents (search, then verify on the official page)
| Need | Search for | Example (sample markets: UK, US, Germany; verified 2026-10-29 where marked) |
|---|---|---|
| Ad transparency coverage | what the Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn libraries show **in that country** | UK/EU: Meta shows targeting and reach; payer EU only [V]. Elsewhere: often active ads only. |
| Company register | "<country> official company register search" | UK: Companies House (free). US: state Secretary of State business search. DE: Handelsregister / Unternehmensregister. |
| Review platforms | "<sector> reviews <country>" | Google Business Profile everywhere; plus Trustpilot, Feefo and sector portals depending on the country. |
| Sector marketplaces | "<product> marketplace <country>" | Cars, property, jobs, travel, food delivery each have their own (find the one buyers actually use in that country). |
| Ad rules for the sector | "<country> advertising rules <sector>", regulator site | e.g. UK: ASA / CAP Code, plus the FCA for credit. Health, alcohol, gambling and credit have extra rules almost everywhere [NV: confirm with the regulator]. |
| Search demand | Google Trends / Keyword Planner set to that country and language | |
| Dominant platforms | local usage reports (e.g. stat sites, regulator media reports) | Some markets lean on platforms not covered here (marketplaces, messaging apps, local search engines). |

## 3. Record and reuse
- Write what you found and verified in the report's "Sources used" with dates.
- If the user keeps a local file (e.g. `references/local.md` or a markets note), add the verified sources there so the next analysis in that market starts faster. Re-verify anything older than ~6 months.

## 4. Competitors in that market
- Find them as a buyer would: search the main keywords in that country and language (incognito, no personalisation), the sector marketplaces, Google Maps for local businesses, and the Ad Library by keyword for the country.
- Pick 3-5 that actually compete for the same buyer and area; say why each was picked. Then use `compare.md`.

## Rules
- Mark every market-specific fact [V] only after reading the official page; otherwise [NV].
- Legal and compliance points are flags for the user to confirm, never legal advice.
