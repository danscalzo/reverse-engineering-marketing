# Personal brand / creator

Goal: understand how content turns into customers. Analyse public work, not private life.

## Specific questions
1. **Channels and the role of each**: where new audience is found (shorts, YouTube, podcast), where it is warmed up (long videos, newsletter), where it is sold to.
2. **Content pillars**: 3-5 recurring themes; format (talking head, podcast clips, text carousels); length; cadence (count the last 30 days, with date).
3. **Hooks**: first 2 lines / 2 seconds of the 10 most viewed pieces; recurring pattern (number, contrarian, story, promise).
4. **Proof**: numbers, clients, books, cases; how they are shown.
5. **CTA and lead magnet**: what the audience is asked to do (link in bio, "comment X", free course, book), where it leads, what it collects.
6. **Offers and value ladder**: free -> low ticket -> high ticket; public prices; ideal customer.
7. **Ads**: Meta and Google libraries on the name and linked domains; what is promoted (content, lead magnet, event) vs what is posted.
8. **Team and system**: who produces (credits, job ads), linked companies, how many pages/domains.

## Sources

## Watch out
- Views and followers are point-in-time: write date and source.
- "Viral" does not mean "sells": the content -> sales link is almost always INFERRED.
